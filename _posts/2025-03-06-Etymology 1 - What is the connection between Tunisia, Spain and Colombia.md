The link between **Cartagena, Colombia**, **Cartagena, Spain**, and the **Carthaginian civilization** comes from their historical connections to the ancient **Phoenician** and **Carthaginian empires**.

- Later conquered by the **Romans** in **209 BC** during the **Second Punic War**.

### 2. **Cartagena, Colombia**

- Founded in **1533** by the **Spanish**, who named it after **Cartagena, Spain**.

- One of the **most important cities in the Americas** during colonial times.

### 3. **The Carthaginian Connection**

- The **Carthaginians**, originally a **Phoenician colony from Tyre** (modern-day **Lebanon**), founded **Carthage** in North Africa (around **814 BC**).
- They expanded across the Mediterranean, including into **Spain**, where they founded **Cartagena, Spain** as a key base.
- After their defeat by **Rome** in the **Punic Wars (264–146 BC)**, **Carthage was destroyed**, and its territories, including **Cartagena, Spain**, were taken by Rome.

### **Summary of the Link**

1. The **Carthaginians** founded **Cartagena, Spain**, naming it **Qart Hadasht** (**New City**) after **Carthage** in Africa.
2. **Spain later founded Cartagena, Colombia**, naming it after their own **Cartagena**.
3. The name **Cartagena** ultimately traces back to the **Carthaginians**, who once dominated the **western Mediterranean**.
4. While **Cartagena, Colombia** has **no direct Carthaginian heritage**, its name is a historical echo of **Carthage’s influence on Spain**.
